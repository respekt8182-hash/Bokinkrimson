# Legal Release Blockers

These facts are intentionally not shown as public placeholders in legal documents. Production validation must provide them through environment variables or confirmed configuration before release.

- `LEGAL_CLAIMS_POSTAL_ADDRESS`: postal address for legally significant messages and claims.
- `LEGAL_PRIMARY_DATABASE_COUNTRY`: country where the primary personal-data database is located.
- `LEGAL_PRIMARY_DATABASE_REGION`: region or data-center location for the primary personal-data database.
- `LEGAL_RKN_NOTIFICATION_NUMBER`: Roskomnadzor notification/registry number, if the operator has one and it is required for publication.
- Confirmed cross-border personal-data transfer status: no public statement is made until the project has a confirmed fact.
